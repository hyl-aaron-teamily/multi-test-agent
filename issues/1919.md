Aiden reports that after chatting with Donny on the macOS client and/or web, many of those messages never appear in the same conversation on the iOS app. The iOS client shows an incomplete conversation history, so the user cannot see messages that already exist on the other clients.

## Steps to reproduce
1. Sign in to the same Teamily account on the macOS desktop client (or web) and on the iOS app.
2. Open the direct conversation with Donny and exchange a batch of messages from the Mac / web side (text plus any rich messages such as images, cards or quoted replies).
3. Open the same conversation on the iOS app and compare its message list against Mac / web.
4. Observed: a large number of the messages present on Mac / web are absent on iOS — the iOS conversation content is incomplete and out of sync.

Every client shows the same conversation content. Messages sent or received on Mac / web propagate to the iOS app in real time, or are reconciled by incremental fetch when the conversation is opened after a reconnect. No message should be missing on iOS, regardless of message type (text, image, card, quoted reply, agent card).

Many messages exchanged with Donny on the Mac / web client never show up on the iOS app: the iOS conversation is missing content and appears out of sync with the other clients.

合并时这几个产物和远端起了冲突，两边各自都是残缺的——我们这份含 seq-gap 修复但不含另一条线的 ARTIFACT 分类功能，远端那份反之。编译产物没有 merge 语义，所以不是二选一，而是用合并后的 SDK（`openim-rust-sdk f81d190`，同时包含两者）重编了一次，并在结果里逐一确认两边的特征串都在。

FFI 导出面未变，因此绑定层（TypeScript / C++ 胶水）未重新生成，也不涉及 ubrn checksum。

## 验证要点（重要，否则会误判为「没修好」）

**存量的错误 memo 不会自动清除。** 修复前被错误标记为「已检查」的 seq 区间仍留在设备的 `local_seq_gaps` 表里，装上新包后**依然会挡住补洞** —— 那些已经丢掉的消息不会自己回来。

所以：

1. **在老设备上验证前，先清除 app 数据并重新登录**，否则验的是被污染的旧状态。
2. 干净状态下复现原场景：iOS 端退出登录或长时间离线，期间在 Mac / Web 上往同一会话发**超过 50 条**消息，再回到 iOS 打开该会话逐条核对。
3. 反复进出会话几次再核对。修复前缺的那段无论进出多少次都补不回来；修复后应在一到两次翻页内补齐。
4. 顺带回归：普通的上翻历史、久未打开的会话首次进入，都不应出现卡顿或缺段。

## 对存量用户的影响（需要产品确认）

**所有在修复前已经丢过消息的设备，仅靠升级拿不回那些消息。** 要恢复，需要在升级时清理一次 `local_seq_gaps` 表（或对该表做一次版本化失效）。这是一个独立于本次修复的决定，建议单独评估——如果确定要做，可以再开一单跟踪。
